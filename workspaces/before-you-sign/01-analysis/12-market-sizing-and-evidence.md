# Market Sizing & Problem Evidence (resolves B1–B4)

## B2 — The CORRECT problem evidence (point-of-sale information asymmetry)
Lead with evidence that is specifically about *not understanding what you bought*,
not scams:

1. **CASE (Consumers Association of Singapore), ~2022: reportedly ~45% of ILP
   policyholders did not fully understand their policy's fee structure before
   purchasing.** ⚠️ **Red-team H3 — PROVENANCE CAVEAT:** this figure currently traces
   only to *secondary* content sources, not a located primary CASE publication. **Do
   not present it as established fact until the primary citation is verified.** It is
   supporting colour, NOT the load-bearing proof — the case below stands without it.
2. **RIY of 1.5–3.0% p.a. is standard for ILPs; cumulative fees can cut returns ~30%
   over 25 years** (MoneySense / industry) — the cost being misunderstood is large.
3. **Fee-only practitioners reject ILPs outright:** Providend has not used ILPs in
   20+ years; MoneyOwl declined to recommend them — expert signal that the
   cost/value gap is real, not a student opinion.
4. **The regulator is acting:** MAS's 2025–26 PHS enhancements (mandated fee
   diagrams, complex-product red banners, pre-transaction alerts) are an official
   admission that disclosure comprehension is inadequate.
5. **FIDReC complaints at a 20-year high** — keep as *context/colour* only; note it is
   partly scam-driven so it is not the core proof (intellectual honesty scores).

## B1 — Market sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM), assumption-explicit
Top-down anchor (sourced): **ILP weighted new-business premium ≈ S$2.5bn/yr** (43% of
S$5.87bn total life NBP, 2024, LIA); ILPs grew **+41% in 2024**, **+31% YoY into
1H2025** — a large and *fast-growing* category.

> **Red-team M3 — honesty on the conversion.** The **defensible anchor is the
> top-down figure** (S$2.5bn ILP weighted NBP; +41% in 2024; +31% YoY into 1H2025 —

## B3 — "Why now" (three converging tailwinds)
1. **Supply:** LLMs make document extraction cheap and instant for the first time.
2. **Demand/regulatory:** record ILP sales + record complaints + MAS disclosure push.
3. **Structural:** commission-driven distribution keeps the information asymmetry in
   place — it won't self-correct, so a neutral consumer-side tool is needed.

**White space (defensible):** a neutral, consumer-side, per-document tool that
quantifies fees + downside *in the user's personal/portfolio context* at the
decision/free-look moment. No incumbent does all of this unconflicted.

**Fast-follower rebuttal (red-team L2 — "why can't Seedly or a robo just build this?"):**
- **Robo-advisers won't** — a fee-exposer cannibalises their own product and
  re-introduces the conflict they'd have to disclose.
- **A content/community site (Seedly) could add a calculator** but lacks the
  combination that is the moat: a deterministic, audited extraction+math engine; a
  persisted personal/portfolio profile; the compliance discipline (no advice verdict);
  and the **free-look-timed funnel**. A loose calculator is not the product.
- **First-mover compounding** — the seeded, growing fee-benchmark corpus and the
  free-look brand are time-and-data moats a fast-follower starts from zero on.
- Honest caveat: none of these is an absolute barrier; the defensibility is *speed +
  unconflicted brand + data accretion*, not a patent. Say so.
