# Core User Flow — "Before You Sign" MVP

## Primary flow: "I was just pitched a product — should I understand it better?"

1. **Land / sign up** → value prop: "Understand the real cost and consequence before
   you sign. We don't sell anything. We don't tell you what to buy."
2. **Upload the document** the user was given (PHS / fact sheet / brochure PDF) —
   or choose "enter details manually".
3. **Extraction + confirm** → system extracts product type, fees, guarantee
   language, lock-in/surrender schedule, key risks, projected return. Shown in an
   **editable form** with confidence flags. *User confirms/corrects.* (No math runs
   on unconfirmed numbers.)
4. **Enter personal context** (short): liquid savings, monthly expenses, horizon,
   stated objective. *(Compliant: used only for neutral arithmetic.)*
5. **Report generated** — the five lenses:
   - **Product Scan**: plain-English meaning of flagged terms; guarantee check
     ("the document states / does not state a capital guarantee; provided by ___").
   - **Fee Lens** (hero): "This will cost you **S$X over Y years** and reduce your
     final value by **Z%**." Gross-vs-net compounding chart.
   - **RiskFit Lens**: "This = **N% of your liquid savings**. A 1-in-20 bad year ≈
     **−S$___**, leaving **__ months** of expenses. Lock-in runs to year __; your
     horizon is __." *Context, not a verdict.*
   - **Decision Gap Checklist**: what the document does *not* answer + **questions to
     ask your adviser**.
   - **Portfolio Mirror** (simplified): concentration & liquidity before/after.
6. **Act**: save report, download PDF, print the "questions to ask" sheet to bring
   to the adviser meeting. Upsell to premium/family at natural points.

## Compliance guardrails embedded in the flow
- Every report header: "General information only. Not financial advice. Not a
  recommendation. Consult a licensed financial adviser."
- No "buy/don't buy", no "suitable/unsuitable", no product ranking-to-purchase.
- All numbers are deterministic and show their assumptions; sliders for return/vol.

## Secondary flows
- **Family review**: same flow, on a product a parent/spouse was pitched.
- **Compare what I hold vs what I'm pitched**: Portfolio Mirror (v2).
- **B2B2C entry**: employer/adviser-provided login → same report flow, co-branded.
