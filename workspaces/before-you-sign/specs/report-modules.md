# Spec — Report Modules (the five lenses)

Authority on the product surface. UX flow in `../03-user-flows/01-core-flow.md`;
compliance in `compliance-guardrails.md`; math in `analytical-engine.md`.

## 01 Product Scan
- In: extracted fields. Out: plain-English glossary of flagged terms + guarantee
  check ("document states / does not state a capital guarantee; provider: ___").
- Safe zone (factual/educational).

## 02 Fee Lens (hero)
- In: confirmed fees + principal + horizon. Out: total cost (S$), fee drag (%),
  gross-vs-net chart, break-even surrender year. (Engine 1.)

## 03 RiskFit Lens
- In: confirmed product + personal context. Out: concentration %, dollar downside
  (Engine 2 P5), liquidity buffer months, lock-in-vs-horizon flag. **Context, not
  a verdict.** (Engine 3.)

## 04 Decision Gap Checklist + Questions to Ask
- In: extracted fields vs canonical checklist. Out: what's missing + a printable
  "questions to ask your adviser" sheet. Highest-value, fully compliant.

## 05 Portfolio Mirror (simplified MVP)
- In: existing holdings (manual) + this product. Out: HHI + liquidity before/after.
  (Engine 4.) Full risk-contribution deferred to v2.

## Every module
- Carries the standard disclaimer; emits no buy/sell/suitability verdict; all
  figures deterministic with visible assumptions.
